Click the screen to type; RT-11 asks the date and the time at boot, Enter skips both. The keyboard follows the MS7004: the host's letters, digits and symbols make the characters on their caps (РУС mode: positional ЙЦУКЕН); arrows, the numeric keypad, F1–F13; Home/Ins/Del/End/PgUp/PgDn are the editing keys; Left Alt = Compose, Right Alt = РУС/ЛАТ. ⛶ (or F11) puts the picture alone on the whole screen, Esc brings the page back; on a touch device ⌨ raises the system's keyboard (letters, digits, Enter, Backspace - Cyrillic letters switch the machine to РУС). "Joystick: on" makes the arrows and Space the joystick on the MS7007 port (SABOT2 on omega-games: J KEMPSTON), and on a touch screen a stick under a finger on the left of the picture and fire on the right. The drives A, B and HD mount like the desktop emulator's: a 400 KB image is one side, an 800 KB one takes both sides of its drive, the HD takes an image of any size (your own, or a new blank one - INIT HD: in the guest). Disks you write to stay in this browser (IndexedDB): "Download" saves a copy, "Revert" returns a shipped disk to its original, "Delete" removes one of yours.
