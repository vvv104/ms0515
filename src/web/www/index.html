Click the screen to type. The keyboard follows the MS7004: the host's letters, digits and symbols make the characters on their caps (РУС mode: positional ЙЦУКЕН); arrows, the numeric keypad, F1–F13; Home/Ins/Del/End/PgUp/PgDn are the editing keys; Left Alt = Compose, Right Alt = РУС/ЛАТ. The drives A, B and HD mount like the desktop emulator's: a 400 KB image is one side, an 800 KB one takes both sides of its drive, the HD takes an image of any size (your own, or a new blank one - INIT HD: in the guest). Disks you write to stay in this browser (IndexedDB): "Download" saves a copy, "Forget" returns a shipped disk to its original, "Delete" removes one of yours. A game on osa.dsk: type R FIST (or open ?disk=osa.dsk&type=R%20FIST).
