MS-0515 emulator - where the disks are

The emulator ships no diskettes of its own.  The disks come from the
software collection, which releases them on their own schedule:

  https://github.com/vvv104/ms0515-software/releases

Download ms0515-disks-<date>.zip (or a single .dsk) and boot one:

  ms0515 --disk0 games.dsk

  games.dsk     OSA 1.0 with its games: Saboteur 2, The Way Of The
                Exploding Fist, Pac-Man and others; the text games are
                on the second side (R DZ2:KING)
  devel.dsk     the Omega development kit: MACRO-11, Pascal, FORTRAN,
                BASIC, KED
  rodionov.dsk  Rodionov's RT15SJ, straight into ROSA Commander
  mihin.dsk     Mihin's OS-16SJ with his formatter and utilities

The collection also has five bare exemplar systems in its systems/
folder, one per monitor build.

A disk of your own: ms0515-disk, beside this file, composes any
combination of a system and bundles of software from a copy of the
collection (git clone https://github.com/vvv104/ms0515-software):

  ms0515-disk compose --repo ms0515-software --list
  ms0515-disk compose --repo ms0515-software --system omega --media dv --add pascal,basico my.dsk

--plan shows where everything would go without writing a disk.

The browser build loads the same disks by itself:

  https://vvv104.github.io/ms0515/
